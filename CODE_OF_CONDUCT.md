# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

## What is expected

- Be civil. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume the other person is doing their best with the information they have.
- Keep it on topic: printers, scanners, Windows, and this code.
- Remember that most people here are debugging something that will not work,
  which is nobody's best moment. Patience costs nothing.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments of any kind — including about someone's
  background, identity, or level of experience.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere else this project is represented.

## Reporting

If something here goes wrong, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@hafeee44-lab](https://github.com/hafeee44-lab), through a GitHub private
report or a direct message. Reports are read and handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts
that break these rules, and will say why when doing so.
